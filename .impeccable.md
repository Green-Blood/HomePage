## Design Context

### Users
This portfolio is for a broad audience: recruiters, clients, collaborators, students, and peers. Most visitors want a quick understanding of who Jey is, what he builds, and whether they should reach out.

### Brand Personality
Confident, grounded, and human. The interface should feel polished and capable without becoming flashy or noisy.

### Aesthetic Direction
Clean editorial portfolio with a balanced amount of motion. The page should feel alive through purposeful transitions and micro-interactions, while keeping content readability first.

### Design Principles
1. Motion should clarify hierarchy and feedback, not distract from content.
2. Keep interactions responsive and tactile with short, smooth easing.
3. Preserve accessibility with robust reduced-motion behavior.
4. Prioritize legibility and scanability over decorative effects.
5. Make key moments memorable: hero intro, portfolio cards, and primary actions.
